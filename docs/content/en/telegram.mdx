## Create the bot

1. Open [@BotFather](https://t.me/BotFather) and send `/newbot`.
2. Pick a name and a username ending in `bot`.
3. Copy the token into `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN`.

## Find your chat id

A bot **can't write to you first**: open your bot in Telegram and press **Start**. Then:

```bash
npx holagram chat-id
```

It lists the chats that wrote to your bot. Copy the id of your private chat into `TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID`. The CLI reads `.env.local` and `.env` from the current folder.

<Callout type="warning" title="getUpdates and webhooks don't mix">
  Telegram only answers `getUpdates` while no webhook is set. If `chat-id` fails after you registered one, run `npx holagram webhook --delete`, then `chat-id`, then register the webhook again.
</Callout>

## Register the webhook

Your replies reach your site through a webhook. Generate a secret, set it as `TELEGRAM_WEBHOOK_SECRET` in your host, deploy, and register:

```bash
npx holagram secret
npx holagram webhook https://your-site.com
```

This calls `setWebhook` with `https://your-site.com/api/holagram/webhook`, your secret and `allowed_updates: ["message"]`. If your route lives elsewhere, pass the full URL ending in `/webhook`. `npx holagram status` shows what Telegram has registered: your URL, `pending_update_count` and the last error, if any.

You do this once per site, and again only if the URL or the secret change.

## Replying to visitors

Everything happens in your private chat with the bot:

- A new conversation arrives as **🆕 New chat #a1b2c3** with the topic, name and email.
- Each visitor message arrives as **💬 #a1b2c3 · Name**.
- To answer, **reply** to any of those messages (swipe ↩️ on mobile, or right click → Reply). The `#a1b2c3` tells you which visitor it is.
- You can also reply to your own earlier answers in the thread: they're linked to the same visitor.
- A message that isn't a reply can't be delivered, and the bot tells you so.

A command (a message starting with `/`, like `/start`) gets a short "connected" confirmation instead.

Replies are plain text: Telegram formatting, photos and stickers don't reach the visitor.
